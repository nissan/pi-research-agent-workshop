# Skill: Decision-ready research brief

Use this skill whenever asked to produce a research brief.

## Steps

1. Restate the decision question in one sentence.
2. Extract evidence from the provided sources only.
3. Group findings into themes.
4. Label each finding as fact, assumption, or recommendation.
5. Identify the strongest uncertainty or risk.
6. End with a concrete next action.

## Quality bar

A good brief helps someone decide what to do next. A weak brief only summarizes information.
